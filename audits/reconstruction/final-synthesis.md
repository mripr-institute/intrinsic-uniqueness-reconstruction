A round trip on a germ does not imply a round trip on all smooth global continuations. Under this reading, composition does prove mutual reconstruction; reachability alone would not prove it. Maximality refers to the retained proof-edge relation: an omitted inverse is not thereby declared mathematically impossible.

The fixed-category assertions have the form \((S,C)\leftrightarrow F_C(S)\), with \(C\) still supplied. A bare spectrum determines the stated unitary spectral class. For Z4, the accepted formula

\[
f_k(t)=(k-1)k^k\frac{t}{(t+k)^{k+1}},\qquad
f_k^{(n)}(t)=(-1)^n(k-1)k^k(k)_n\frac{t-n}{(t+k)^{k+n+1}}

has a strictly nonzero prefactor and denominator on \(t>0\). It therefore gives exactly one zero, at \(t=n\), for every \(n\geq1\). The zeroth derivative is positive on this open domain; it must not be included in a claim that every zero set is \(\{n\}\). For finite \(k\), the polynomial tail differs from \(t e^{-t}\), so the countermodel genuinely breaks uniqueness in the stated class.

\[
