## Mathematical disagreements and their resolution

|---|---|
| A spectral cycle seemed to reconstruct any stationary density | The maps do not form a left inverse on Gamma3: forgetting to its spectrum and completing fixed J selects Gamma2. Keep bare spectrum outside intrinsic identification; retain full coordinate realization or explicitly identify the candidate mixing measure |
| Generic Legendre advice required both closedness and convexity | The exact target exposes every positive point with a coercive uniquely minimized comparison. Either lsc or convexity gives equality; the narrower theorem survives independent proof |
| Centered CGF was said to recover an arbitrary uncentered Poisson law | \(N\) and \(N+k\) have the same centered CGF. The centered function still reconstructs \(I\); retain shift \(+1\) to identify the named uncentered law |
| A unit Todd series was treated as normalized | A unit only requires \(q_0\ne0\). For every \(q_0=c\ne0\), triangular recursion satisfies every positive-degree equation. Explicit \(Q(0)=1\), or degree zero, is essential and is now present in every registry/graph interface |
| Joint deficit data were described without explicitly matching the observed target | Every admissible potential has some linked law/involution. The exact clause now requires both observations to equal the prescribed Sigma pair and concern the same candidate \(J\) |
| A formal/local countermodel acquired a global source after quotienting | The graph first forgets the analytic-global candidate restriction into `GERM_ONLY`; its counterexample targets arbitrary smooth continuation. Quotient edges retain original source/target IDs and signatures |
| Intrinsic graph edges named unspecified placed derivative data | Derivative hierarchy, exact zeros, closure jet and integer samples are now explicitly intrinsic at \(t>0\), \(t=1\) or positive integers. Placed variants retain \(P\) |

## New Phase IV reductions and evidence

The strengthened Bernstein tail theorem removes every finite initial sample. Equilibrium inversion removes a candidate smooth-density assumption. Scale-invariant Bregman reconstruction removes a global second-derivative/convexity premise; recentering transports the single calibrated second derivative. Every placed-code collision is proved to obstruct multiplication. The original exact-zero-set problem is refuted by Z4.

The adversarial closure audit also supplied the missing conditional inverse at the existing self-decomposition node, P9: one independent linked decomposition at a contracting scale identifies Gamma(2,1). Characteristic-function telescoping proves this for an arbitrary real-valued probability candidate, so support and moments are derived; the residual atom recovers the scale. The original-law linkage remains explicit. This inverse has been independently checked, and its full-family forward construction is distinguished from identifying a selected individual original law. These changes close existing inverse questions and do not enlarge the mathematical neighbourhood.

The Lean record reports 69 checked exported theorems, of which 53 are new. It includes concrete converse kernels and all per-theorem axiom reports. Analytic/operator/topological/spatial and global irredundancy proofs beyond those signatures remain written proofs. The independent graph scripts check references, retained contexts, bidirectional reachability and quotient projection; those are architecture checks, not substitutes for the local mathematical proofs.

The full report, proof record, minimality theorem, graph, diagnostics and formal record are frozen together after final graph review. The earlier master manuscript and earlier audit files are not overwritten.
